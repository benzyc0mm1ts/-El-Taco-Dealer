Frequently Asked
Questions

Our doors are open most of the week — here's the full schedule:

  • Tuesday – Thursday: 5:00 PM – 11:00 PM
  • Friday – Saturday: 5:00 PM – 2:00 AM (Late Night Fiesta nights)
  • Sunday: 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM (Brunch & afternoon service)
  • Monday: Closed — our team rests and preps for the week

Kitchen last call is typically 30 minutes before closing. We recommend arriving early on weekends as the place gets packed fast.

Yes — we deliver! Here's everything you need to know:

  • Own riders: Available within a 5 km radius of our location, Mon–Sun during operating hours
  • Third-party apps: Also available on GrabFood and Foodpanda for wider coverage
  • Minimum order: ₱350 for own-rider delivery; check apps for their own minimums
  • Estimated time: 25–45 minutes depending on traffic and distance
  • Packaging: All orders are packed hot and sealed to keep everything fresh on arrival

Tap the motorcycle icon in the bottom right corner of this page to start your order.

We make it easy to secure your spot:

  • Facebook: Message us at our official El Taco Dealer page — fastest response time
  • Phone: Call or text us directly during operating hours for same-day bookings
  • Walk-ins: Always welcome on Tuesday–Thursday and Sunday with no wait most nights
  • Advance booking: We strongly recommend reserving 24–48 hours ahead for Fri & Sat evenings
  • Group bookings (6+): Please book at least 3 days in advance so we can prepare your table setup

For special occasions like birthdays or anniversaries, let us know when booking and we'll make it extra memorable.

Absolutely — every single tortilla, every single service. Here's our process:

  • Masa: Ground daily from 100% non-GMO heirloom corn — no masa harina, no fillers
  • Press & cook: Hand-pressed and finished on a seasoned cast-iron comal throughout the entire shift
  • Freshness window: Tortillas are served within minutes of leaving the comal — never pre-stacked
  • Gluten-free: Our corn tortillas are naturally gluten-free — just ask your server to confirm for your specific order

This is one of our proudest commitments. If you've ever had a truly fresh corn tortilla, you'll never go back.

We've got plenty of love for plant-based eaters! Here's what's available:

  • Roasted Mushroom Taco: King oyster & shiitake blend, smoked paprika oil, pickled onion
  • Charred Nopal Cactus: Grilled nopal paddle, salsa verde, queso fresco (skip cheese for vegan)
  • Black Bean & Chipotle: Slow-cooked heirloom black beans, chipotle crema, avocado
  • Seasonal Veggie Special: Ask your server — we rotate a plant-based special weekly
  • Vegan-friendly: Most of our salsas and sides are vegan — just confirm with your server on toppings

Our corn tortillas are naturally vegan and gluten-free, making them a perfect base for any plant-based build.

Here's the breakdown on spending requirements:

  • Tuesday – Thursday & Sunday: No minimum spend — order as much or as little as you like
  • Friday & Saturday (reserved tables): Minimum consumption of ₱800 per person, covering food and drinks
  • Friday & Saturday (walk-in bar seats): No set minimum, but drinks are required for bar seating
  • Private event packages: Custom minimum applies based on group size and setup — contact us for a quote

The ₱800 minimum on weekends goes a long way — a couple of tacos, a drink, and a side and you're well above it.

We absolutely love hosting and can accommodate a wide range of events:

  • Birthdays & Celebrations: Private or semi-private area, custom decoration setup, birthday taco platters
  • Corporate Events: Team dinners, client entertaining, company milestones — we handle the logistics
  • Full Venue Buy-Out: Available on select Sundays and Mondays for exclusive full-restaurant events
  • Custom Menus: Work with our chef to curate a tasting menu or family-style spread for your group
  • Drink Packages: Cocktail towers, mezcal flights, open bar options — all available for events
  • Live Entertainment: DJ, live acoustic, or band packages available upon request

Get in touch at least 1 week in advance for small groups (up to 20) and 2–3 weeks for larger events.

We keep payment flexible so you never have to worry:

  • Cash: Always accepted — exact change appreciated but not required
  • GCash: QR code available at every table and at the counter
  • Maya (PayMaya): Accepted at point of sale and via QR
  • Credit & Debit Cards: Visa and Mastercard accepted (minimum ₱200 for card transactions)
  • Delivery via GrabFood / Foodpanda: Payment handled within the app — card, GCash, or COD depending on option
  • Split bills: We're happy to split — just let your server know before ordering

No surcharges on any payment method. What you see on the menu is what you pay.
